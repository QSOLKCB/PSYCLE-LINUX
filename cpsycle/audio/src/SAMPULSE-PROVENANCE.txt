Sampulse / XMSampler provenance record

Scope
-----
This record applies to the C-Psycle Sampulse / XMSampler implementation under
`cpsycle/audio/src/xmsampler*.c` and `cpsycle/audio/src/xmsampler*.h`.

Psycle distribution terms
-------------------------
The imported C-Psycle XMSampler source and headers carry the Psycle project
notice permitting redistribution and modification under the GNU General Public
License, version 2 or (at your option) any later version (`GPL-2.0-or-later`).
The complete GPL version 2 text is shipped at `cpsycle/COPYING`.

Satoshi Fujiwara / XMSampler lineage
------------------------------------
The imported source explicitly states that Sampulse is "Partially based on
Satoshi Fujiwara 'XMSampler'". Upstream `cpsycle/AUTHORS` separately records
Satoshi Fujiwara <sfpg@users.sourceforge.jp> among authors whose source code or
modifications are used by Psycle.

Historical Psycle XMSampler source in the upstream project lineage also carries
the Psycle `GPL-2.0-or-later` project notice. Satoshi Fujiwara has publicly
written that his XMSampler work was subsequently continued by other Psycle
developers and became the Sampulse machine:

https://www.sfpgmr.net/blog/entry/aodix-tracker-%E3%81%AB%E3%81%A4%E3%81%84%E3%81%A6%E5%B0%91%E3%81%97%E8%AA%9E%E3%82%8B.html

This record preserves Satoshi Fujiwara's attribution and the distribution terms
carried by the Psycle implementation; it does not erase or replace any earlier
independent authorship.

OpenMPT IT-filter lineage
-------------------------
The imported source also states: "IT Filtering from OpenMPT."

OpenMPT's official project history records that the full ModPlug Tracker source
was opened in 2004 and that OpenMPT used `GPL-2.0-or-later` during the historical
period from which this Psycle implementation was developed, before OpenMPT was
later relicensed under BSD terms. See:

https://openmpt.org/legacy_software

The OpenMPT-derived filtering lineage is therefore recorded here as historical
`GPL-2.0-or-later` source provenance. The complete GPL version 2 text is already
shipped at `cpsycle/COPYING`.

Packaging rule
--------------
Source and binary distributions that include Sampulse must preserve:

- the existing GPL notice in the XMSampler source/header files;
- Satoshi Fujiwara's XMSampler attribution;
- the OpenMPT IT-filter attribution; and
- this provenance record or equivalent third-party attribution documentation.

No DSP or sampler behaviour is modified by this Phase 1 provenance restoration.
